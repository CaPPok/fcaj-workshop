Event 3

Field Report: “FCAJ x Agentic AI Build Week Hackathon 25/07/2026”

Event Objectives

This is the weekly networking and exchange event of the FCAJ program, taking place every Saturday. This is also the third event I have attended since joining FCAJ. However, this session features the presence of teams from the Agentic AI Build Week Hackathon to share their work.

  • Presentations on the projects by the teams participating in the Agentic AI Build Week Hackathon.
  • Sharing of the project deployment process and encountered challenges.
  • Guidance on finding and brainstorming project topics.
  • Sharing of practical experiences and teamwork methods by each team during the competition.

List of Speakers

There are a total of 5 teams presenting their products at the event. The participating teams include:

  • One Team
  • Plan V Team
  • Signal Scout
  • Six Pillars Team
  • 3KA Team

Additionally, there is a special appearance by Mr. Giuseppe Marazzotta - Head of Tech & Solution Architecture ASEAN at AWS, along with inspiring stories.

Key Highlights

I am especially interested in two projects: SA Professional Native App and KFC Bot Agent.

SA Professional Native App

  • The team built an AI-native application designed for Solution Architects. This application has the ability to analyze natural language and structured project requirements, outline high-level hybrid-cloud aware architecture options that comply with company standards. It also automatically generates editable Draw.io diagrams using official AWS architecture icons, provides directional AWS cost estimates for the region, and aggregates recommendations, assumptions, and requirement gaps through a chat sidebar with project-specific custom instructions.
  • The system leverages Amazon Bedrock models, Knowledge Base for pricing retrieval, Draw.io MCP, and AWS Pricing MCP to output requirement catalogues, architectures, diagrams, and cost estimate tables.
  • Transforms the workflow from manually reading line-by-line BRD documents and guessing to uploading documents and chatting naturally — generating requirement catalogues, initial architecture drafts, automated IaC code, and cost estimates within minutes.
  • The team’s demo is well-executed. They proved that not every large and complex product is a good product; the ability to solve new problems is what makes a product special.

KFC Bot Agent

  • Builds an ordering system directly within chat frames via Zalo OA, Messenger, and future channels without switching apps, creating new accounts, or repeatedly explaining. Core operations follow the model: Goal $\rightarrow$ Plan $\rightarrow$ Tools $\rightarrow$ Act $\rightarrow$ Verify.
  • The system on AWS includes an Ingestion Layer, AgentCore Runtime, Tool Layer, Memory Layer, Data & Assets Layer, along with Observability & Security services.
  • The solution delivers clear economic efficiency with a cost of only $0.006 per order (calculated based on 500 orders/day), a total infrastructure cost of about $88/month, fast response latency ranging from 3-5 seconds, and up to -60% reduction in infrastructure code by using AgentCore to replace the traditional infrastructure layer.
  • The team demoed the project excellently, demonstrating the ability to solve practical problems and deliver clear economic value. This is a first-prize-winning project in the Agentic AI Build Week Hackathon.

What I Have Learned

  • Practical AI Application Development: Gained deep insights into how teams leverage generative AI and multi-agent systems to automate complex professional workflows like cloud architecture design and conversational ordering automation.
  • Problem Solving and Project Workflow: Learned how to identify heavy operational bottlenecks in enterprise processes and transform them into viable AI-integrated software solutions under the time pressure of a product-building week.
  • Inspiration from Teams: Absorbed valuable lessons on teamwork, ideation, and execution from the participating competition teams.

Event Experience

This event provides an extremely dynamic and inspiring environment, showcasing the incredible creativity and execution speed of student teams during the Agentic AI Build Week.

Atmosphere

The event atmosphere is filled with enthusiasm as teams present practical solutions built during the competition. Listening to the journey of the teams provides a clear view of how modern AI applications are shaped and materialized.

The fact that the teams answered questions and inquiries from participants like me created an interactive learning environment where theoretical knowledge was combined with practical experience. This helped me better understand how ideas can be applied in real-world scenarios.

Practical Technical Insights

Observing how theoretical cloud architecture concepts are integrated with LLM agents, automated diagram generation tools, cost calculation, and multi-channel conversational bot systems narrowed the gap between classroom knowledge and industrial engineering standards. Honest sharing regarding deployment hurdles and teamwork strategies was extremely practical.

Community Connection

The event once again affirms the value of being part of the FCAJ community, providing a collaborative space to exchange ideas, learn from experienced mentors, and other participants.
