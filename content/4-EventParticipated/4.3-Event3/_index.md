### Key Highlights

I am especially interested in two projects: **SA Professional Native App** and **KFC Bot Agent**.

#### SA Professional Native App

- The team built an AI-native application designed for Solution Architects. This application has the ability to analyze natural language and structured project requirements, outline high-level hybrid-cloud aware architecture options that comply with company standards. It also automatically generates editable **Draw.io** diagrams using official AWS architecture icons, provides directional AWS cost estimates for the region, and aggregates recommendations, assumptions, and requirement gaps through a chat sidebar with project-specific custom instructions.
- The system leverages Amazon Bedrock models, Knowledge Base for pricing retrieval, Draw.io MCP, and AWS Pricing MCP to output requirement catalogues, architectures, diagrams, and cost estimate tables.
- Transforms the workflow from manually reading line-by-line BRD documents and guessing to uploading documents and chatting naturally — generating requirement catalogues, initial architecture drafts, automated IaC code, and cost estimates within minutes.
- The team's demo is well-executed. They proved that not every large and complex product is a good product; the ability to solve new problems is what makes a product special.

#### KFC Bot Agent

- Builds an ordering system directly within chat frames via Zalo OA, Messenger, and future channels without switching apps, creating new accounts, or repeatedly explaining. Core operations follow the model: **Goal** $\rightarrow$ **Plan** $\rightarrow$ **Tools** $\rightarrow$ **Act** $\rightarrow$ **Verify**.
- The system on AWS includes an Ingestion Layer, AgentCore Runtime, Tool Layer, Memory Layer, Data & Assets Layer, along with Observability & Security services.
- The solution delivers clear economic efficiency with a cost of only **$0.006 per order** (calculated based on 500 orders/day), a total infrastructure cost of about **$88/month**, fast response latency ranging from **3-5 seconds**, and up to **-60% reduction in infrastructure code** by using AgentCore to replace the traditional infrastructure layer.
- The team demoed the project excellently, demonstrating the ability to solve practical problems and deliver clear economic value. This is a first-prize-winning project in the **Agentic AI Build Week Hackathon**.
