# Field Report: "Event Day - 13/06/2026"

### Event Objectives

This was the weekly networking and exchange event of the FCAJ program, taking place every Saturday. This was also the very first event I attended since joining FCAJ.

- Connect and network with everyone in FCAJ.
- Share practical experience regarding projects.
- Corporate culture in reality.
- The journey of learning and personal development.

### List of Speakers

The speakers at the event included:

- **Mr. Danh Hoang Hieu Nghi**, currently an AI Engineer - AWS Community Builder – AWS Student Builder Group Leader, sharing about his journey from the First Cloud AI Journey to becoming an AWS Partner.
- **Mr. Pham Thanh Dat**, currently working at Colgate, sharing his journey of becoming a Data Analyst.
- **Mr. Cuong**, also currently working at Colgate, with a special topic on "Corporate Culture".
- **Mr. Truong Hoang Trong** sharing perspectives and key concepts of DevOps.
- **Dinh Trung Kien, Nguyen Minh Tho** sharing the process of building a project named **A scalable URL shortening service on AWS**.

### Key Highlights

#### Corporate Culture

All speakers brought extraordinary perspectives with their own distinctive topics. However, the presentation by Mr. Cuong revolving around **Corporate Culture** left the deepest impression and reflection on me. Without diving into overly complex technical jargon, the sharing opened up a completely new mindset regarding how an organization operates and how the younger generation should prepare themselves when stepping into the job market.

- **Vision of Organizational Culture:** History and reality have proven that strong corporations or peak production models like the Toyota Production System all lay their foundations on stability, standardization, and strict discipline. Culture is the core factor that helps major enterprises like AWS or multinational corporations maintain their leading position through various fluctuations.
- **The Three-Dimensional Value Model of Human Development:** Based on the "Doing the Right Work" philosophy, the presentation connected the dimensions of human life through three pillars: **Doing Human Work**, **Doing Professional Work**, and **Doing Civic Work**.

#### Perspective on DevOps

- The role of a DevOps Engineer encompasses a massive ecosystem with numerous pressures, ranging from handling system incidents, managing security, optimizing cloud costs, to acting as a bridge between departments.
- A comprehensive look at the DevOps Tool Landscape, detailed from planning, development, and testing to operation and monitoring.
- Guided learning paths for beginners: starting from Linux, Networking, basic programming, Git, to understanding containerization and building small projects to practice hands-on troubleshooting.

#### A scalable URL shortening service on AWS

- Building a URL shortening system with high scalability capabilities.
- Designing architectures and distributing services on the AWS platform.
- Demonstrating how the system handles massive traffic loads, helping visualize the bridge between theoretical knowledge on paper and a complete, real-world product.

### What I Have Learned

- **Real-world Career Perspectives:** Gained a clearer understanding of development roadmaps, job requirements, and necessary skills for positions such as Data Analyst, AI Engineer, and DevOps Engineer directly from industry practitioners.

- **Cloud System Design Mindset:** Grasped how to approach a real-world problem, from designing scalable architectures on AWS to organizing and teamwork in a technology project.

- **The Importance of Corporate Culture:** Recognized that culture is not just a tool for retaining talent but also the foundation determining the longevity of an organization, thereby helping me better orient myself in choosing a suitable working environment in the future.

### Event Experience

This event brought me a lot of impressions and special emotions, as it was the first time I attended an in-person gathering with the FCAJ community.

#### Open Networking Environment

The speakers not only conveyed technical knowledge but also shared numerous real-life stories, interview experiences, and career orientations for students.

#### Professional Organization

Despite the large scale of the event with a massive number of attendees, every detail—from preparing facilities and managing the timeline to supporting participants—was handled meticulously and professionally by the FCAJ mentor team.

The topics were presented clearly, combined with practical examples that made it easy for attendees to comprehend and apply the knowledge to their personal projects. Furthermore, mentors were always ready to answer questions and provide detailed guidance for those interested in specialized topics.

#### Connection Atmosphere

The event was not only a place to learn technical knowledge but also a wonderful opportunity to connect with peers who share the same passion for technology, boosting my confidence significantly as I take my first steps into the cloud community.
