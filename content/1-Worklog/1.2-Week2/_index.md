### Week 2 Achievements:

* **Basic understanding of the AI/ML ecosystem:** Understood how AWS segments AI/ML service layers and the central role of Amazon SageMaker in developing custom models instead of only using ready-made APIs.
* **Understanding SageMaker:** Clearly distinguished between two important task flows used in the project:
  * **Processing Job:** Used for background periodic retraining workflows.
  * **Real-time Endpoint:** Used to host the model 24/7 for instant predictions for Backend services.
* **MLOps design mindset:** Understood the necessity of building a CI/CD flow for Machine Learning, including quality gates before replacing an old model with a new one.
* **Expanded knowledge in Data and Analytics:**
  * Learned methods for collecting, processing, and storing large-scale data.
  * Understood the role of a Data Lake compared with a traditional Data Warehouse.
